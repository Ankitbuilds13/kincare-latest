Help & FAQ

Learn how CareBridge keeps your family safe.

How does the SOS button work?

The one-tap emergency SOS button immediately alerts the assigned helper, the adult child, and a registered local emergency contact simultaneously[cite: 1, 2].

How do I retain control of tasks?

CareBridge is built on a Dignity & Control engine[cite: 2]. The Elder View features an "Approve Task" button which must be clicked before the proxy can begin the job[cite: 2].
